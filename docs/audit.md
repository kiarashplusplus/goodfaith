# Good Faith program: internal security review

This is an internal review. An independent audit is recommended before any mainnet deployment.

## Scope
`programs/goodfaith` (Anchor 1.2.1), all twenty instructions (v2: person-level eligibility, issuer-signed registration and wallet replacement, timelocked settings, wind-down and sweep), `gateway.rs`, checked against `docs/spec.md`. The `app/` directory is out of scope.

## Method and tools
- `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` (clippy 0.1.99): clean.
- `cargo audit` 0.22.2: no vulnerabilities. Five "unmaintained" warnings in transitive dependencies (ansi_term, bincode 1.3.3, derivative, libsecp256k1, paste), all pulled in by the Solana and Anchor stacks.
- Manual line-by-line review of every instruction: signers, owners, PDA seeds and stored bumps, account substitution, duplicate mutable accounts, arithmetic, close handling, permissionless paths, stale state.
- Civic gateway layout re-checked against `identity-com/on-chain-identity-gateway` (`solana/program/src/state.rs`).
- Tests added after the review (59 total, including the v2 paths: issuer-signed registration, one person one wallet in both directions, wallet replacement with escrow refunds to the new wallet on repay and default, retired wallets, the default policy both ways, the timelock lifecycle including the timelock changing itself and freeze discarding a pending change, wind-down gates, cancellation window, successor validation and sweep, a frozen pool keeping an announced wind-down, issuer suspension and restoration, the `active_loans` counter): the error paths the first pass left to `is_err` are now asserted by code, including `LoanNotActive` (reached by naming a live token account as the closed escrow, since Anchor deserialises before it checks constraints), account substitution (rent recipients, token owners, vault) by Anchor code, initialise/register twice, a member PDA from another pool, zero-escrow default, late repayment before default, and malformed gateway bytes.
- Property tests: Trident (stable 0.12.0, 0.13.0-rc.4 pre-release) was not attempted. It needs its own harness and fuzz workspace and its Anchor 1.2.1 support is unconfirmed, so I wrote proptest 1.x property tests over LiteSVM 0.18.1 instead (`props::invariants_hold`, 24 random sequences of up to 40 operations over a pool with a three-day timelock, including scheduled and applied settings, cancellations, wallet replacements, wind-down announcements and cancellations, sweeps, issuer suspension and clock jumps). Run more cases with `PROPTEST_CASES=...`.

## Findings
| ID | Severity | Description | Status | Test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GF-01 | Medium | `cosign_loan` did not bind the co-signer's signature to the terms they saw. The borrower could cancel and re-request a larger amount or term before the co-signer's transaction landed, and the pool authority could raise the escrow rate in the same window. | Fixed: `cosign_loan(expected_amount, expected_term_days, max_escrow)`; new errors `RequestMismatch`, `EscrowTooHigh` | `audit_cosign_binds_to_approved_terms` |
| GF-02 | Medium | `update_pool` changed `grace_days` retroactively. The authority could set it to 0 and make open loans defaultable at once, seizing co-signer escrow. | Fixed: grace period is stored on the loan at open (`Loan.grace_days`) and `mark_default` uses it | `audit_grace_period_is_fixed_at_open` |
| GF-03 | Medium | Token-2022 mints (transfer fee, transfer hook, permanent delegate, pausable, etc.) were accepted and would break vault and escrow accounting. | Fixed: `initialize_pool` accepts only a classic SPL Token mint and the classic token program | `audit_pool_rejects_token_2022_mints` |
| GF-04 | Low | `repay` failed with a duplicate-mutable-account error when the co-signer repaid from the same token account that receives the escrow refund. | Fixed: `dup` on `cosigner_token` (the two transfers run in order, so it is safe) | `audit_lowering_limits_mid_loan_keeps_loan_repayable`, `audit_escrow_topup_and_prefunded_pdas_do_not_block` |
| GF-05 | Low | Pool PDA is `["pool", mint]`, so the first caller of `initialize_pool` for a mint becomes its authority and fixes the gatekeeper network. A squatter could pre-empt the official pool. | Accepted (spec-mandated seeds). Mitigation: initialize in the deployment transaction batch; the app must pin the canonical pool address and check `authority` and `gatekeeper_network`. | none |
| GF-06 | Info | Mints with a freeze authority (devnet USDC's mint has one, `CJtyoKSLrktozQzjERTiK3btQtiTK3nN4QrqGHLidyCT`; mainnet USDC too) let the stablecoin issuer freeze the vault, an escrow or a user's wallet, blocking loans, repayments or defaults. The program cannot prevent this; the white paper states it. | Accepted, residual risk; documented | none |
| GF-07 | Info | The authority can change the settings (`gatekeeper_network`, `max_loan`, `max_term_days`, `grace_days`, `max_guarantees_per_member`, `cosigner_escrow_bps`, `issuer`, `timelock_seconds`) for future loans, but only through `schedule_params` → `apply_params` after `timelock_seconds` (7 days at launch), so every change is public before it applies. It cannot move funds, except to the announced successor after a wind-down with no open loan. Lowered limits never block repayment of an open loan. `suspend_issuer` is the one instant action and only removes a power. | By design | `timelock_lifecycle`, `audit_lowering_limits_mid_loan_keeps_loan_repayable`, `audit_frozen_pool_cannot_be_updated_or_refrozen` |
| GF-08 | Info | Authority handover. `propose_authority` records a pending key; only that key can `accept_authority`; the current authority can cancel; `freeze_pool` clears the pending key and any scheduled settings so a frozen pool can never be changed through stale state. The zero key and the current authority are rejected as proposals. The handover grants no new power: the new authority inherits exactly GF-07. | By design | `authority_handover_is_two_step`, `freeze_clears_pending_handover` |
| GF-09 | Info | Fee sponsor (Kora, off-chain). The sponsor's per-transaction cap must cover a first co-sign (borrower token account + Loan + escrow rent, 6,507,600 lamports with the v2 Loan); and sponsoring token-account creation for arbitrary recipients would let anyone farm rent. Mitigations are app-side: `max_allowed_lamports = 8_000_000`, `max_signatures = 3` (wallet + issuer + sponsor on a registration), the Checking page makes the sender pay when the recipient has no token account, and the `/api/sponsor` proxy holds the credentials, allow-lists three methods and rate-limits per client (Kora 2.0.5's CORS is allow-any). | Mitigated in app | `scripts/kora-e2e.ts` |
| GF-10 | Medium | Issuer trust. The issuer key decides which person hash a wallet carries and can move a person to a new wallet without the old wallet's signature. A compromised issuer colluding with a wallet could (a) register the same human under two hashes, defeating uniqueness, or (b) rebind an eligible person's wallet to one it controls and collect that person's future escrow refunds. It cannot move pool funds, open loans, or change settings. | Mitigated: the issuer is changed only through the timelock, `suspend_issuer` stops it at once, every registration and rebind is an event (`MemberRegistered`, `WalletRebound`) the operator monitors, retired wallets can never be reused, and the key lives in the identity service, separate from the authority. Residual risk accepted for v2. | `register_binds_wallet_to_person_and_needs_the_issuer`, `rebind_rejections`, `suspending_the_issuer_stops_joins_until_a_new_issuer_is_set` |
| GF-11 | Low | Issuer rotation lag. Replacing a lost or leaked issuer key takes `timelock_seconds`; in that window nobody can join or replace a wallet (if suspended) or the old key still works (if not suspended). | Accepted: suspend first, then schedule the new key; existing members keep borrowing, co-signing and repaying meanwhile. | `suspending_the_issuer_stops_joins_until_a_new_issuer_is_set` |
| GF-12 | Low | Successor accounting. After a wind-down takes effect, the vault can only shrink by sweeps to the announced successor and only once `active_loans == 0`; repayments of the last loans still land in the vault first and are swept afterwards. The successor is validated as a token account of the pool mint not owned by the pool PDA (so not the vault or an escrow), but the program cannot know whether the successor is a sound destination: that is the authority's timelocked, public decision. `total_swept` keeps the vault equation exact. | By design | `wind_down_lifecycle`, `frozen_pool_keeps_an_announced_wind_down`, `props::invariants_hold` |
| GF-13 | Info | Civic discontinued Civic Pass verification in July 2025. The on-chain check is unchanged (the Identity.com gateway program still runs), but the tokens are now issued on the fund's own gatekeeper network by its identity service, after a provider check (Persona). The program trusts whatever that network's gatekeeper issues, exactly as it trusted Civic. | Documented; the issuer service is in `app/` scope, not this review's | none |

### Reviewed with no issue found
- Signer, owner and `has_one`-style checks: every authority path is a `Signer` plus a key constraint (`pool.authority`, `pool.pending_authority`, `request.cosigner`, `request.borrower`, `loan.*`). `freeze_pool` sets `authority = None` and `pending_authority = None`; `accept_authority` requires a pending key, so nothing can set an authority on a frozen pool: freezing is irreversible.
- PDA seeds use the canonical bump stored at creation (`pool.bump`, `loan.bump`, `loan.escrow_bump`, `member.bump`, `request.bump`).
- Account substitution: vault is pinned to `pool.vault`; escrow is a PDA of the loan; every token account is constrained by mint and authority; rent recipients are pinned to the stored `rent_payer`; the mint account must match the pool PDA seeds; token program is `Interface<TokenInterface>` (only the two real token programs).
- Duplicate accounts: borrower and co-signer are different people (the request checks `cosigner_member.person != borrower_person`, the co-sign checks the two Person keys); `payer_token` cannot be the vault or escrow (their authority is the pool PDA, which cannot sign).
- Person and Member: both are `init` at registration, so a hash cannot get a second wallet and a wallet cannot get a second person; Members are never closed and `rebind_wallet` creates the new Member with `init`, so a retired wallet can never come back under any person. Persons are self-seeded by their stored hash and pinned to the wallet that signs (request, cosign) or to the loan (repay, default), so no Person can be substituted. The escrow refund and default leftovers follow `cosigner_person.wallet`, never the stale `loan.cosigner`.
- Timelock: `schedule_params` validates, `apply_params` validates again before applying; `params_effective_at` uses the timelock in force at scheduling; `freeze_pool` discards pending settings; `apply_params` has no signer so the operator's cranker or anyone can apply, which only ever enacts what the authority announced.
- Wind-down: the gate is `now >= wind_down_at`; `announce` refuses a second announcement, `cancel` refuses once in effect, `sweep` requires effect, `active_loans == 0` and the exact successor. `active_loans` is incremented at cosign and decremented at repay-in-full and default (checked math, never below zero).
- Arithmetic: checked everywhere; the escrow ceiling is computed in u128 and is at most the loan amount because bps <= 10000.
- Close handling: loan accounts are never closed, loan ids never repeat, so escrows cannot be revived. Request accounts use Anchor `close`. Pre-funding a loan or escrow PDA with lamports, or sending extra tokens to an escrow, does not block cosign, repay or default (extra tokens go to the co-signer or the vault by the live balance).
- Permissionless calls: third-party repay and `mark_default` only move funds along the spec'd paths. Tiny repayments cost the caller only.
- Stale `LoanRequest`: eligibility, gateway tokens, limits, vault balance and active-loan state are re-checked at cosign time.

### Civic gateway (`gateway.rs`)
Verified against upstream: `GatewayToken` is Borsh with no discriminator, in this order: `version u8`, `parent_gateway_token Option<Pubkey>`, `owner_wallet`, `owner_identity Option<Pubkey>`, `gatekeeper_network`, `issuing_gatekeeper`, `state` (1 byte: Active=0, Frozen=1, Revoked=2), `expire_time Option<i64>`. Options use a 0/1 tag byte. `version` is always 0 and unused upstream. Expiry is only in `expire_time`; there are no other feature flags in the account. Token address derivation upstream is `find_program_address([owner, "gateway", 8-byte additional seed (zeros by default), network], gateway program)`. The program id in `gateway.rs` is the Civic one. The code does not recompute the PDA: it requires the account owner to be the gateway program and checks `owner_wallet` and `gatekeeper_network` from the data, which is equivalent to upstream's check and also accepts tokens issued with a non-zero seed. Trailing bytes are ignored. One small difference: upstream treats a token as valid when `now == expire_time`; this program requires `expiry > now` (stricter by one second, matching the spec). I could not fetch the integration crate source to re-confirm its exact check order, so that part rests on the account layout above.

## Interface changes
- v2 (no deployed accounts, no migration): `Pool` += `issuer`, `timelock_seconds`, `pending_params`, `params_effective_at`, `wind_down_at`, `successor`, `active_loans`, `total_swept`; `PoolParams` += `issuer`, `timelock_seconds`; new `Person` account; `Member` is now `{wallet, person, retired, bump}`; `Loan` += `borrower_person`, `cosigner_person` (appended, 221 bytes); `register_member(person_hash)` takes an `issuer` signer and a `person` account; new `rebind_wallet`, `schedule_params`, `cancel_params`, `apply_params`, `suspend_issuer`, `announce_wind_down`, `cancel_wind_down`, `sweep_to_successor`; `update_pool` removed; `request_loan` takes `borrower_person` and `cosigner_member`; `cosign_loan`, `repay`, `mark_default` take Person accounts instead of Members; eight errors appended (`WalletNotBound` … `InvalidSuccessor`); events `WalletRebound`, `ParamsScheduled`, `ParamsCancelled`, `IssuerSuspended`, `WindDownAnnounced`, `WindDownCancelled`, `SweptToSuccessor`; `MemberRegistered` and `LoanOpened` carry person keys; `LoanDefaulted` carries `cosigner_barred`.
- Added after the review: `propose_authority(new_authority)`, `cancel_authority_proposal()`, `accept_authority()`; `Pool.pending_authority: Option<Pubkey>` (appended, +33 bytes); `PoolParams::validate` bounds `max_term_days <= 3650`, `grace_days <= 365`; events `AuthorityProposed`, `AuthorityProposalCancelled`, `AuthorityChanged`; `PoolUpdated` now carries `params`; `Repaid` and `LoanDefaulted` carry `pool`, `loan_id`, `borrower`, `cosigner`. No deployed accounts existed, so no migration.
- `cosign_loan(expected_amount: u64, expected_term_days: u16, max_escrow: u64)`: new arguments (GF-01).
- `initialize_pool`: `token_program` is now fixed to the classic SPL Token program and `mint` must be a classic mint (GF-03). Account order unchanged.
- `Loan` has a new `grace_days: u16` field (before `status`), 2 bytes larger; regenerate the IDL and client types (GF-02).

## Invariants tested (`props::invariants_hold`, checked after every operation)
1. Vault balance == total_donated + total_repaid + total_recovered - total_lent - total_swept.
2. Token conservation: vault + escrows + every wallet that ever held tokens (current and retired) + the successor equal the supply handed out.
3. For every loan, repaid <= principal; Repaid loans have repaid == principal; the borrower and co-signer persons differ.
4. While a loan is Active its escrow account exists and holds exactly `loan.escrowed`; closed loans have no open escrow; `pool.active_loans` equals the number of Active loans.
5. Per person: `active_guarantees` equals the number of Active loans they co-signed (never underflows); `active_loan` matches the single Active loan they borrowed, and no person has two; `person.wallet` is the actor's current wallet, whose Member is not retired and points at that person; every replaced wallet's Member is retired.
6. Settings only change through `apply_params` (the live settings always equal the last applied `PoolParams`, with `suspend_issuer` zeroing the issuer); a pending change always validates.
7. Once a wind-down is in effect, `loan_count` and `total_donated` never move again and the state never leaves wind-down; a sweep only happens with no Active loan.
8. Default policy: every Defaulted loan's borrower person is ineligible; when the shortfall exceeded the escrow, the co-signer person is ineligible too.

The property test assumes no tokens are sent straight to the vault or an escrow; those are covered by a separate unit test.

## Residual risks
- GF-05 and GF-06 above.
- The authority is trusted within the timelock (GF-07): every change is visible for `timelock_seconds` before it applies, and anyone may then apply it. Freezing stays optional; a wind-down needs a live authority to announce.
- Sybil resistance is only as strong as the issuer service's identity check and its person hash (GF-10, GF-13); the program enforces one wallet per hash, not one hash per human.
- The successor named in a wind-down is trusted (GF-12).
- A co-signer and borrower who collude can walk away with escrow-sized losses borne by the fund; the escrow is a deterrent, not full cover.
- Direct token transfers into the vault are not counted in `total_donated`.
- Dependencies carry unmaintained-crate warnings (see above); no known vulnerabilities at review time.
- Not fuzzed with Trident or coverage-guided tools; the property test is bounded.

## Statement
This is an internal review by the development team, not an independent audit. An independent audit is recommended before mainnet.
