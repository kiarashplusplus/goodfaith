Good Faith

Interest-free, fee-free loans between neighbours, funded by gifts. You pay back exactly what you borrow, and a friend you trust co-signs.

Before you start: what you need

  • A Texas resident, 18 or older. Your government ID must be issued in Texas; we use that as a proxy for where you live.
  • A government ID and a selfie for a one-time check with our verification partner, Persona. Good Faith keeps only a keyed fingerprint of the ID so one person holds one membership; your documents are never stored by us or put on the blockchain.
  • A Solana wallet such as Phantom or Solflare. You don't need any SOL: a fee sponsor pays network fees.
  • To borrow, a co-signer: a different person who has also verified, and who puts down a deposit (below).
  • To turn USDC into cash, a MoneyGram identity check of its own, and the same ID at the counter. To pay cash in, also your Social Security number.
  • Optional: an email address for notices about your membership, such as a recovery request or a bar.

One membership per person, one wallet per membership. A lost wallet can be recovered without losing your membership; it takes a few days and you are told first.

Borrowing

  • First loan up to 150 USDC. Each loan repaid on time that ran at least 30 days raises your limit by 100, at most once every 30 days, up to 500. A loan that is not repaid resets it.
  • No interest and no fees: you repay exactly what you borrowed, any amount, from any wallet.
  • Terms up to 180 days, then 30 days of grace.
  • For your first 30 days as a member (and after a wallet recovery or an unpaid loan) you stay on the first-loan limit.
  • A request nobody co-signs expires after 7 days.

Co-signing

  • You put down 25% of the loan as a deposit, held in escrow.
  • If the loan is repaid on time, the deposit is yours again in full. If not, it covers what is missing, and comes back only if the borrower later pays back.
  • You can guarantee at most 2 loans at a time.
  • If your deposit didn't cover everything and the fund lost money, you can't borrow or co-sign for 1 year, ending sooner if the fund is paid back.

Only co-sign for someone you trust. A guarantee is a real commitment and can strain a friendship.

If you can't repay

  • Paying back late, after the grace period: no new loans for 90 days, counted from when you pay back.
  • Not paying back: no new loans for 2 years. Paying back later shortens it.
  • Lateness never costs money: there is no interest and no fee, ever.

Cash at MoneyGram

Cash-out and cash-in powered by MoneyGram, from the app's Checking page.

  • Cash out: turn USDC into US dollars at a MoneyGram location. MoneyGram checks your name, phone, date of birth, address and government ID, then gives you a reference number to collect the cash with that ID.
  • Cash in: pay cash at a MoneyGram location and receive USDC, for example to repay. MoneyGram also asks for your Social Security number and a second ID.
  • Fees: MoneyGram charges its own fee and shows it before you commit; Good Faith adds nothing. Its test environment quoted $3 on a cash-out. To confirm: production fees
  • Where: MoneyGram's test environment offers Texas pickup locations. To confirm: production coverage in Texas, cash-out and cash-in

How it works

  • Donors give USDC to a shared fund as gifts: they earn nothing and cannot take it back, which lets the fund lend at zero cost.
  • The rules are a public program on the Solana blockchain. Settings changes are announced ahead of time, and anyone can check what is deployed.
  • Your money stays in your own wallet until you donate it or put down a co-signer deposit. The fund and the deposits are held by the public program under its rules, not by a person.
